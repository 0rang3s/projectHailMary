Ice-only reference date (RCM ARD, 2025-04-01 23:02 ascending, same pass as May 7 / May 19 / Aug 7).
Shows the river frozen everywhere (98% overall, 98% upstream) four weeks before the emergency: no ice-jam pattern.
Flood numbers are NOT computed for this date: in early April the land is snow-covered, and wet snow / frozen
bog surfaces look dark on radar, so the water cutoff flags ~185 km2 of "water" that isn't water.
Those same dark inland patches appear on Apr 30, which is why the Apr 30 inland red patches are likely not flood.
No stats.json here on purpose, so the app's date slider skips this date.
