Ice jam flooding is harder to predict than open water flooding. Whether a jam forms, and where it forms along a river, depends on local ice and river conditions that are difficult to forecast ahead of time.

Ice jam floods can happen very quickly. Water levels near a jam can change fast, so a community beside the river may have little warning between normal conditions and rising water.

Ice jam floods are often localized, because a jam affects the stretch of river around where it sits, and they can occur anywhere along a river where ice becomes blocked.
