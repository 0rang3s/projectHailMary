In 1957 the federal government relocated Kashechewan onto a floodplain on the James Bay coast, so the community has long been built on land that river water can reach when the Albany River rises.

An engineering firm hired by the community in 2015 assessed its dike, which was then about 24 years old, and found that it was deteriorating and posed a risk to the community that the firm judged to be unacceptable.
