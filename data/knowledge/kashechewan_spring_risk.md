Kashechewan is threatened by the waters of the Albany River every spring, although the community does not always flood, so a spring evacuation or warning does not by itself mean a flood has happened.

The federal government has said it remains committed to relocating Kashechewan because the community faces regular flooding events and annual evacuations that disrupt people's lives.
