In its April 30, 2026 update the Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo said water levels had lowered slowly, and that an ice run coming from the Town of Athabasca had moved closer to the ice jam near Fort McMurray but had not yet reached it.

The municipality said the Draper evacuation alert remained because water levels could affect egress and access on Garden Lane and River Bend Close. Residents in the alert area did not have to leave, but were asked to be ready to leave on short notice.

Provincial river forecasters flew over the river daily, and the update said the ice jam near Fort McMurray had melted back by about 5 kilometres since the previous day's flight. Warm temperatures and sunshine were helping the ice break down gradually within Fort McMurray.
