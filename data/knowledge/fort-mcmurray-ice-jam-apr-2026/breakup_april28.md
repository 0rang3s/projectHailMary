On April 28, 2026 the Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo reported that an evacuation alert remained in effect for Draper, because an ice jam on the Athabasca River north of Fort McMurray was raising water levels and causing overland flooding in low-lying areas.

The municipality said the ice jam was about 25 kilometres long, extending upstream past the Fort McMurray Golf Club, and that it had raised water levels at Fort McMurray by nearly 7 metres. Water levels on the Clearwater River were also rising at a slow but steady pace.

The municipality described the overland flooding as common during river breakup and listed closed areas that included the Snye parking lot and Morrimoto Drive. It expected the jam to stay in place and melt, since no ice runs or runoff waves were arriving, with snowmelt increasing as temperatures warmed.
