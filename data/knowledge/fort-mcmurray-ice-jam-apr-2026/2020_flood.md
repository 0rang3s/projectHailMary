In April 2020, flooding caused by ice jams on the Athabasca River forced the evacuation of downtown Fort McMurray. The municipality issued mandatory evacuation orders for the lower townsite, blocked access to the downtown area, and issued a boil water advisory.

CBC reported that ice jams in the Athabasca pushed water in the opposite direction and raised levels on the Athabasca, Snye and Clearwater rivers, that the Waterways neighbourhood south of downtown was fully submerged, and that the municipality asked Ottawa for military help.
