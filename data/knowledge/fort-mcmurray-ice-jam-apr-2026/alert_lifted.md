A Canadian Press report said the evacuation alert for Draper was lifted after officials said ice had broken up on the Clearwater and Athabasca rivers, and that the municipality no longer saw a significant risk of ice jams.

The municipality said water rose briefly on the Clearwater River when an ice jam upstream of Fort McMurray broke free, then fell quickly after a small jam at the mouth of the river released. It warned that several ice runs were still expected over the following days and asked people to keep a safe distance from riverbanks.
