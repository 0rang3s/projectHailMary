source: Report about Draper evacuation and residents being told to leave

On April 28, 2026 the Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo issued an evacuation alert for more than 130 residents of Draper, a community about 12 kilometres southeast of Fort McMurray along the banks of the Clearwater River, because of an ice jam and the risk of flooding.

The report said the ice jam was causing river levels to rise, which the municipality feared could cause overland flooding in low-lying areas, and that no evacuation order had been issued so far. An alert means residents should prepare to leave, not that they must leave.
