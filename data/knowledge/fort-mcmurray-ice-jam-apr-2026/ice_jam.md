CBC's April 2026 coverage described Fort McMurray as having a long history of flooding, caused mainly by ice jams at the confluence of the Athabasca and Clearwater rivers, and noted that spring thaw each year brings a risk of ice jams as river ice flows north.

The same report said provincial river forecasters had issued an ice jam watch and that the jam near Fort McMurray was about 25 kilometres long. Ice jams form where moving river ice stalls and piles up, which can raise water upstream of the blockage.
